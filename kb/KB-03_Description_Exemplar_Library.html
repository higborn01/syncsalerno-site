KB-03 Description Exemplar Library

KB-03: Description Exemplar Library

Catalog Copilot Knowledge Base | Team-authored | v1.0, September 2026

How to Use This Library

These sixteen pairs show Catalog Copilot what an approved description looks like, four per category. Each one starts with the attributes the GPT would be given, then the kind of seller copy we see in real listings, then the approved version and a note on what changed and why. The notes matter more than the copy. A model that only sees good output learns to imitate it; a model that sees the reasoning learns which rule to apply to the next product.

The products and brand names (Fernhollow, Oakline Studio, Pantry & Pine, Harlow Lane) are invented and modeled on the categories in KB-02. The "before" text is written to show common problems; it is not copied from any real listing. Two exemplars (the water bottle and the tote bag) keep an inference in the approved copy on purpose, with a note saying it should have been flagged, so the GPT sees that approved copy is not automatically perfect.

Home goods

Exemplar 01: Ceramic table lamp

Part Content
Attributes given brand: Fernhollow | material: ceramic base, linen shade | color: Sage | dimensions: 12 x 12 x 22 in | bulb: E26, 60W max, not included | cord: 6 ft
Before (seller copy) STUNNING Fernhollow lamp!!! This luxurious ceramic lamp will elevate any room in your home. Perfect for bedrooms, living rooms, offices and more. High quality and built to last. A must-have!
After: title Fernhollow Ceramic Table Lamp with Linen Shade, 22 Inch, Sage (61 characters)
After: bullets

Size: 22 in tall with a 12 in shade, sized for a nightstand or end table.

Materials: Glazed ceramic base with a linen drum shade.

Bulb: Takes one E26 bulb up to 60 watts; bulb not included.

Cord: 6 ft cord reaches most outlets without an extension.
After: description This lamp gives a bedside table or reading corner a soft, even light. The sage glaze is muted enough to sit next to wood or white furniture, and the linen shade keeps the bulb from glaring.
After: alt text Sage green ceramic table lamp with a white linen drum shade on a wooden nightstand.
What changed Removed five banned words (stunning, luxurious, elevate, perfect, must-have) and the exclamation points. "Built to last" had no support in the data, so it became material facts. Bulb limits were in the attributes and went into a bullet because they answer a real question.

After: title Fernhollow Waffle Weave Cotton Throw Blanket, 50 x 60 Inch, Oatmeal (67 characters)

Part Content
Attributes given brand: Pantry & Pine | diameter: 10 in | material: aluminum with ceramic nonstick coating | oven safe: 400 F | dishwasher: hand wash recommended | cooktops: gas, electric (not induction)
Before (seller copy) Non-toxic ceramic pan, PFOA free, healthy cooking with no oil! Dishwasher safe. Works on all stovetops.
After: title Pantry & Pine Ceramic Nonstick Skillet, 10 Inch (47 characters)
After: bullets

Size: 10 in, sized for eggs and single portions.

Coating: Ceramic nonstick over aluminum.

Care: Hand wash recommended.
After: description An everyday pan for eggs, pancakes, and quick sautés. Hand washing keeps the nonstick coating working longer.

What changed This one had four problems. "Non-toxic" (T1-02), "healthy cooking" (T1-01), "dishwasher safe" contradicts the data, and "all stovetops" is false for induction. "PFOA free" could be true but is not in the attributes, so it was flagged for the merchandiser to confirm.

Before (seller copy) Keeps drinks cold longer than any other bottle! 100% leakproof guarantee. #1 rated water bottle.

After: bullets
