KB-11 Human Review Rubric and Decision Log

KB-11: Human Review Rubric and Decision Log Template

Catalog Copilot Knowledge Base | Team-authored | v1.0, September 2026

Why This Exists

Catalog Copilot drafts; people decide. This rubric is how a reviewer makes that decision the same way every time, and the decision log is how the retailer can later show who approved a description and why. That record is the difference between strong and weak augmentation in practice: the GPT changes how the work is done, but a named role is still accountable for every description that goes live.

Roles

Role Responsibility
Category merchandiser Supplies product attributes, runs Catalog Copilot, fixes missing data, completes the first rubric pass
Brand reviewer Resolves every Tier 2 flag from KB-09, gives final sign-off, owns the published copy
Catalog Copilot Drafts, self-checks against KB-01 and KB-09, and explains its choices; never approves anything

Scoring Rubric

Each draft is scored 1 to 4 on five criteria. A 4 means publish as written, a 3 means minor edits, a 2 means real rework, and a 1 means the draft fails on that criterion.

Criterion 4 3 2 1
Accuracy (matches the attributes) Every fact traces to the source data One minor wording drift, no wrong facts A fact is overstated or a needed attribute is missing without a flag Any invented fact, number, or feature
Compliance (KB-09) No flags, or all flags resolved n/a (compliance is pass or fail at 4 or 1) n/a Any Tier 1 claim present, or an unresolved Tier 2 flag
Voice (KB-01) Reads like the guide; no banned words One or two word swaps needed Tone is off in several places Reads like generic advertising
Format and platform (KB-01, KB-05, KB-07) Title, bullets, paragraph, and alt text all within limits One element slightly over length or out of order Several format problems Title over 150 characters or structure ignored
Usefulness (KB-08) Answers the shopper's first questions up front Answers them, but not in the best order Leaves a key question unanswered Mostly adjectives, little information

Decision Rules

Decision When
Approve Compliance is 4 and every other criterion is 3 or higher. Minor edits are made during review and logged.
Edit Compliance is 4 and at least one criterion is a 2. The merchandiser revises (or regenerates with notes) and the draft is scored again.
Reject Compliance is 1, or Accuracy is 1. The draft is discarded, and the cause is logged so the prompt or knowledge base can be fixed.

Rejections are the most useful rows in the log. If the same rule keeps failing, the fix belongs in the knowledge base or the GPT instructions, not in another round of manual edits.

Decision Log Template

One row per reviewed draft. Reviewers are recorded by ID, not by name or email, so the log can be shared for analysis without exposing who did the work.

Field What to Record Example
log_id Sequential log number L-0142
review_date Date of the decision 2026-10-06
sku_id Product identifier B07XYZ1234
version_id Draft version B
draft_source copilot_draft or copilot_edited copilot_draft
reviewer_id Anonymized reviewer ID R-03
score_accuracy ... score_usefulness Five rubric scores, 1 to 4 4, 4, 3, 4, 3
flags_raised KB-09 rule IDs the GPT flagged T2-01
flags_resolution Approved, qualified, or removed, per flag T2-01 removed
decision approve, edit, or reject approve
edit_pct Approximate share of the draft the reviewer changed 12%
notes Why, in one sentence Removed "eco-friendly"; no certification on file
sign_off Brand reviewer ID for anything published R-01
