Cuban

John Doe’s Cubanos

A three-day cubano, brined one day, marinated the next, and cooked (and eaten) on the third.

Ingredients

  • — Day 1: brine —
  • 4 cups orange juice
  • 3.5 cups water
  • 1/2 cup apple cider vinegar (or rice wine vinegar)
  • 1/2 cup spiced rum (optional, but good)
  • 1/2 cup salt
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • Lots of minced garlic
  • 1 tbsp each fresh thyme, rosemary, oregano, sage (rough chopped)
  • Ground pepper, 3 bay leaves
  • — Day 2: mojo marinade —
  • 2/3 cup olive oil
  • 2/3 cup cilantro, rough chopped
  • 4 tbsp mint leaves
  • 1/2 cup orange juice
  • 1/2 cup fresh-squeezed lime juice
  • 7 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 tbsp grated orange peel
  • 2 tsp fresh oregano, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2/3 tsp black pepper
  • 2/3 tsp fine sea salt
  • — To build the sandwich —
  • 9-inch baguette loaves
  • Yellow mustard
  • Swiss cheese
  • Dill pickles
  • Butter, thickly sliced ham

Method

  1. Day 1: Combine all brine ingredients in a crock-pot container. Submerge the pork and refrigerate.
  2. Day 2: Remove the pork from the brine and pat dry. Mix all the mojo ingredients in a 2-gallon zip-top bag and add the meat.
  3. Day 3: Roast the meat in the mojo at 425°F for 20 minutes, then baste. Lower to 325°F, baste every 30 minutes, and check the temperature after 2 hours (then every 20 minutes) until it hits 170°F.
  4. Let rest 30 minutes — do not taste yet. Then taste, and marvel at how delicious it is.
  5. Thinly slice, then grill. Layer from the bottom up: grilled pork, griddled ham, Swiss cheese, sliced pickles — mustard on the bread, press, and grill until the cheese melts.
