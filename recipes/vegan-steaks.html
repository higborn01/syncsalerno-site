Vegan Steaks

Ingredients

  • — Wet —
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce (60 ml)
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 tablespoon liquid smoke
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon miso
  • 1 1/2 cups hot vegetable broth (350 ml)
  • — Dry —
  • 2 1/2 cups vital wheat gluten (320 g)
  • 1 tablespoon onion powder
  • 1 tablespoon garlic powder
  • 1/3 cup chickpea flour (40 g)
  • 1 cup cooked lentils, rinsed and drained (140 g)
  • 2 tablespoons Kitchen Bouquet browning sauce

Method

  1. Combine the dry ingredients in a food processor.
  2. Add the wet ingredients and blend until a dough forms.
  3. Blend in the browning sauce.
  4. Shape into steaks.
  5. Steam 20 minutes per side.
  6. Finish on the grill with steak spices.
