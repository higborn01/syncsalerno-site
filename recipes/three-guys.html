“Three Guys” Sandwich

“Really I just used the Heinz gravy as a base and added wine and lemon, salt, and pepper until it tasted like Three Guys.”

Ingredients

  • Sub rolls
  • Dry mozzarella (Polly-O)
  • Ham, on sale and domestic
  • 3 cans Heinz chicken gravy
  • 1 packet powdered brown gravy
  • 3/4 cup Marsala wine or dry white wine
  • Lemon, salt, and pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Assemble the sandwiches: mozzarella on both sides, ham in the middle. Cut into 1-inch slices and lay in an oven-safe pan.
  2. Warm the Heinz chicken gravy to a simmer. Add the wine and reduce, then stir in the remaining gravy.
  3. Mix the powdered gravy with the leftover wine to make it easier to incorporate, then whisk in.
  4. Season with lemon, salt, and pepper to taste — until it tastes like Three Guys.
  5. Pour the gravy over the sandwiches in the pan and bake at 425°F until bubbling in the middle.
