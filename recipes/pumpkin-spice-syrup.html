Pumpkin Spice Syrup

Ingredients

  • 2 1/3 cups brown sugar (466 g)
  • 1 1/6 cups pumpkin purée (273 g)
  • 1 3/4 cups water (414 g)
  • 2 1/3 tsp pumpkin pie spice (7 g)
  • 2 1/3 tsp vanilla bean paste (12 g)
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk (397 g)
  • A slightly larger pinch of salt (2 g)

Method

  1. Add everything to a saucepan.
  2. Heat on medium, whisking until smooth.
  3. Simmer 5–7 minutes until glossy and slightly thickened.
  4. Cool and refrigerate. Yield: about 1,571 g (roughly 4 standard 14 oz cans) of syrup.
